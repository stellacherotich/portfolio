A free checklist for anyone who builds or commissions courses

Before You Publish: 12 checks for online training

Answer yes or no for each one. Small details decide whether a course works for every learner. Each one is quick to fix once you know where to look.

Download the one-page PDF

Structure

  1. The course opens with one sentence on what learners will be able to do afterwards.
  2. Headings follow a clear order: one title, then sections, then sub-sections.
  3. Learners can always see where they are and how much is left.

Content

  1. Sentences are short enough to read on a phone, and any jargon is explained.
  2. Every image that carries meaning has a text description.
  3. Nothing relies on colour alone, such as "fix the fields in red".
  4. Videos have captions and a transcript.

Interaction

  1. Every drag, drop or hover can also be done with a tap or the keyboard.
  2. Feedback explains why an answer is right or wrong, including the wrong ones.
  3. The course can be completed with a keyboard alone, in a sensible reading order.

Connectivity

  1. Pages load on a slow connection: small files, no autoplay video.
  2. Key content has a download or offline version.

Your score

  • 10 to 12 yes. Ready to publish.
  • 7 to 9 yes. Needs a quick pass.
  • Below 7. Worth a rework.
