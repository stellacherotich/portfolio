Lesson 1

Welcome

Learn to find a client's real need before you recommend a product.

Learning Outcomes

By the end of this module, you will be able to apply the Ask, Probe, Confirm routine in a first client meeting to identify their main protection need before recommending a product.

To get there, you will:

  • Differentiate between open, closed, and leading questions.
  • Demonstrate the routine by choosing the strongest response at each stage of a client.
  • Justify why you confirm a client's priority before you recommend a product.

This module takes about 5 minutes and works on your mobile device. You will learn a three-step routine, practice choosing questions, and handle a first client meeting at Msingi Life. Msingi Life is a fictional company created for this sample.

Lesson 6

Summary

This course emphasized the importance of understanding client needs through effective communication strategies.

  1. 1

    Prioritize inquiry. Begin by asking questions to uncover client needs.

  2. 2

    Listen actively. Pay close attention to clients' responses to understand their concerns.

  3. 3

    Tailor recommendations. Provide advice that aligns with the insights gathered.

  4. 4

    Understand question types. Differentiate between open, closed, and leading questions.

  5. 5

    Confirm understanding. Reflect clients' priorities back to ensure clarity.

  6. 6

    Practice empathy. Acknowledge clients' emotions to build trust.

  7. 7

    Observe non-verbal cues. Pay attention to body language and expressions for deeper insights.

By applying these principles, you can enhance your client interactions and provide meaningful, tailored solutions.
