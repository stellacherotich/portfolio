Hi, I'm Stella, an instructional designer based in Nairobi, Kenya, where I design learning that people can use on the job.

I create inclusive and accessible learning experiences that help teams learn and do their work well. I'm also Vice Curator of the Global Shapers Nairobi Hub and building VolunTia, a platform that connects skilled volunteers with NGOs.

About

Before learning design I taught English in Japan and studied environment and development, then moved into data science and curriculum work at Moringa School. I like the place where knowing something and teaching it meet. Plenty of organisations have people who know exactly how things work, and that knowledge lives in their heads. I help turn it into clear material that frontline teams can pick up and use.

My work has covered technical curriculum, training for NGO partners and operational documentation, designed for learners with patchy internet, small budgets and real consequences when a guide is unclear. Along the way I've shipped 6+ programmes end to end, reached 450+ learners, earned an NPS above 50 across flagship programmes, and managed up to three programme portfolios at once.

Whatever I'm building, my goal is the same:

  • Turn expert knowledge into clear, usable material
  • Design for the real conditions learners work in
  • Test with real people and keep improving after launch
