About

Before learning design I taught English in Japan and studied environment and development, then moved into data science and curriculum work at Moringa School. Today I design from Nairobi, serve as Vice Curator of the Global Shapers Nairobi Hub, and am building VolunTia, a platform that connects skilled volunteers with vetted NGOs.

I like the place where knowing something and teaching it meet. Plenty of organisations have people who know exactly how things work, and that knowledge lives in their heads. I help turn it into clear material that frontline teams can pick up and use.

My work has covered technical curriculum, training for NGO partners and operational documentation. I've designed for learners with patchy internet, small budgets and real consequences when a guide is unclear.

I'm most excited by organisations building their knowledge systems from scratch, where good design now keeps paying off for years.

What I do

How I work

  1. Discover

    I start by learning who the learners are, where they will use this and what success looks like for them.

  2. Design

    Next I map out the outcomes, sequence and structure, and I can explain the reason behind every choice.

  3. Develop

    Then I build it and test it with instructors, subject experts or a small pilot group before it goes out.

  4. Scale

    Finally I document the standards, support the facilitators and set up feedback loops, so the programme keeps improving.
